ShortsStudio

ShortsStudio is a private, self-hosted tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Teto's Terrific Recipes. It produces short cooking videos on the creator's own computer and uses the YouTube Data API only to upload those videos to the creator's own channel, to schedule their publication and to check their processing status.

ShortsStudio is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in with Google, and no data about other people is collected.
